## Policy and Procedures

### Objectives:

At the end of this episode, I will be able to:

1. Identify common policies and procedure documentation.

Exam Objective: *2.7 - Explain the importance of asset management and documentation.*

Description: In this episode, the viewer will identify common policies and procedure documentation such as business impact analysis, service level agreements, mean time between failure, mean time to recover, recovery point objective and recovery time objective.

-----------------------------------------------------------

*  Company policies and procedures
	+ Business impact analysis \(BIA\)
	+ Mean time between failure \(MTBF\)
		- MTBF is used to anticipate how likely an asset is to fail within a certain time period or how often a certain type of failure may occur.
	+ Mean time to repair, recover/restore \(MTTR\)
		- repair - the time it takes to fix a broken system/component
		- recover/restore - the time it takes to return systems/services to operational status
	+ RPO vs. RTO
		- RPO is the time from the last data backup until an incident occurred [that may have caused data loss]
		- RTO is the time that you set to recover the lost data.
		- RPO - how much data can we lose \(based on last backup\) 
		- RTO - what is the max duration of time, following the event
	+ SLAs and uptime requirements
		- Protect your business,
		- managing expectations
		- mitigate the potential for disputes.
